The local URDF is a visual-only assembly of the upstream distal wrist and jaw
meshes. Its joint origins and axes follow urdf/Si/psm_tool.urdf.xacro from the
same source revision. It is not a validated dynamics or collision model and
must not be used as a medical-device controller.
